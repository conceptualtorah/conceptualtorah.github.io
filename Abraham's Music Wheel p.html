Chord: None
How to use the wheel: Set the Scale by rotating the lower and middle wheels until the note from the bottom wheel that you want to be the root shows up in the window of the middle wheel that is labeled the root of the mode you want (Major or Minor). After the Scale is set, rotate the upper wheel (the Chord Wheel) so that the arrows are pointing to notes within the scale. Read the Chord name by seeing which note is being pointed to by the arrow labeled 'Root', and the mode (Major or Minor) by seeing which arrow next to the label '3rd' is pointing to a note within the scale.
